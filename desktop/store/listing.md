## English (en-US)

**Product name:** Al-Jami' Hadith Encyclopedia

**Description:**

Al-Jami' — the Encyclopedia of the Prophet's Hadith, on your computer and working offline.

More than 339,000 hadiths in 245 source books, with biographies of over 30,000 narrators and what the imams of hadith criticism said about them, takhrij, commentaries, rare vocabulary (gharib) and subject indexes. The six canonical collections follow the editions referenced by al-Maktaba al-Shamila: Sahih al-Bukhari (Sultaniyya edition), Sahih Muslim (Turkish edition), Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami' al-Tirmidhi (ed. Bashar Awwad Ma'ruf) and Sunan al-Nasa'i (Egyptian edition), with their text, vowelling and numbering.

Download the encyclopedia once (about 1.6 GB) and the whole app works without a connection: reading, searching texts and openings, chains of narration and narrator pages. It updates itself whenever a newer edition of the data is published.

- Full-text search in the texts and their openings, and lookup by hadith number in every book.
- Chains of narration with every narrator linked to their biography and critics' verdicts.
- Takhrij, parallel narrations, text comparison, supporting and corroborating reports.
- The complete tree of chains, from the first narrator to the compilers.
- Hadith gradings as summarised by al-Durar al-Saniyya, attributed to each scholar.
- Several tabs in one window, back and forward, copy link.
- Arabic interface, dark mode, printed or Harf numbering.

No ads, no account required. The interface and texts are in Arabic.

- Six canonical books on al-Maktaba al-Shamila's editions
- 30,000+ narrator biographies with critics' verdicts
- Takhrij, commentaries and rare vocabulary
- Complete tree of chains of narration
- Search texts, openings and hadith numbers
- Updates itself, no ads
